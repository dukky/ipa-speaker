IPA Speaker

Paste an IPA transcription and hear it. Runs entirely on your device.

Try

Use from the iPhone share sheet
  1. In the Shortcuts app, create a shortcut and turn on Show in Share Sheet, accepting Text.
  2. Add URL Encode on the Shortcut Input.
  3. Add Text: https://dukky.github.io/ipa-speaker/web/?ipa= followed by the URL Encoded Text variable.
  4. Add Open URLs.

Then select IPA anywhere, tap Share, pick the shortcut, and tap Play.
